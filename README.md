# BT Transport Management — Fleet Compliance (M1 setup files)

These three Excel files are the starter templates for your Microsoft 365 database.
Download each one, then follow the guide to create your SharePoint lists from them.

- 1-Operators.xlsx — your 4 operator companies
- 2-Vehicles.xlsx — vehicles (sample fleet, replace with your own)
- 3-Trailers.xlsx — trailers (sample, replace with your own)
